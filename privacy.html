Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Who we are

Session Ready is a product of Fractal Company, based in Brazil. For any privacy question or request, write to lucas.digikal@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you buy, we receive your name, email address, country and the details of your order. Payment is handled by Stripe, which collects your card details under its own privacy policy. We never see or store your full card number.

When you write to us, we keep your message and email address so we can answer you.

Why we use it

We use this information to deliver your files, answer support questions, process refunds, prevent fraud and keep the records the law requires. We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the services that make the sale work, such as Stripe for payments and our email provider for delivery and support, with Meta to measure our ads (see below), and with authorities when the law requires it.

Cookies and browser storage

This site stores a small value in your browser to run the offer timer.

We use the Meta Pixel on this site and the Meta Conversions API after checkout so we can tell which ads lead to purchases. The Pixel records page visits and clicks on the buy buttons. When you complete a purchase, we send Meta the order value, the products bought, your country and your email address in hashed (scrambled) form, along with your IP address and browser type. Meta uses this under its own privacy policy. You can limit ad tracking in your Meta ad settings or by blocking cookies in your browser.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Support messages are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you by writing to the email above. We answer within 30 days.
